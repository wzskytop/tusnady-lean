# r65 submission-snapshot scope audit

## Manuscript binding

The supplied r65 PDF and TeX are preserved byte-for-byte under `verification/`. Their hashes are recorded in `manuscript-r65.json` and checked before and after verification. They are not PDF outputs generated by Lean or by this build.

This snapshot is based on the verified r56 all-transition union project. The r65 main theorem statement is identical to r56. Comparing the theorem, lemma, proposition, and fact environments finds one wording addition: the conditional gain is stated to hold almost surely. The principal numerical bounds and their quantifiers are unchanged. The actual Lean conditional statements use the appropriate almost-everywhere conventions.

The version-specific Lean interfaces and explicit statement checks now refer to r65. Verification runs again on these source files and the bound r65 manuscript inputs. No r56 success record is relabeled as r65 evidence.

## Proven conclusions and proof-model differences

1. The main theorem covers all A>0, all n≥1, iid uniform points, and all sample-dependent colorings simultaneously. Its deterministic lower-bound consequence is also proved. No unproved probability estimates are supplied as assumptions.
2. The main dependency chain uses all-good-transitions geometry: h*(48/b)^(n/2). The simultaneous interface proves the paper's exact threshold and failure constants for even b≥4. Single/all-transition geometry interfaces cover b≥3 without a parity assumption.
3. The local gain uses the existing even-base average-before-maximum/representative/Rademacher argument. The paper's full arbitrary-base midpoint/symmetric-jump proof is not substituted into the final chain. An existential power-of-two base suffices for the main theorem.
4. Midpoint comparison and a finite-law moment implication are separately checked. The full arbitrary-real-random-variable version of Fact 2.2 is not asserted to be formalized.
5. The internal potential uses finite vertical probes and a proved reduction to continuous rectangle counts. No equality with the paper's continuous-height potential is claimed. The separate centered-error estimate refers to this finite-probe process; the main proof uses compensated loss.
6. The cited upper bound, historical claims, bibliography, figures, AI methodology narrative, and higher-dimensional heuristic are outside Lean's verification scope. The source comparison is not a fresh exhaustive manual proof or priority audit.
7. Fact A.1 in r65 states measurability and integrability. The finite-digit case needed by the formal proof is covered, not its broadest general formulation.

## Reproducibility and trust boundary

The verifier records the exact Git commit, environment, pinned dependency revisions, source and manuscript hashes, all local theorem axiom reports, eight statement checks, and every kernel replay exit code. It also traverses actual proof terms to require the all-transition union route and exclude sequential-crowding, half-of-stages Markov, and older global Laplace reductions from the main theorem.

Fresh local or GitHub runs produce their own `verification/current-run.json`. CI attaches its logs and checked package to its run. The package checker compares the current source and record hashes, requires complete successful replay, and archives the same bytes it checked. Hashes establish consistency, not authorship or external attestation. A reader can inspect the displayed theorem types and independently rerun Lean.

The correct scope statement is: **a Lean formalization of the main lower-bound theorem in the r65 manuscript, with the documented alternate local proof and finite-probe model.** It is not full-PDF or line-by-line proof verification.
